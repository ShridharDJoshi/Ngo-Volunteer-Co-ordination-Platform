How It Works

1

Register & Join

Create your account as a volunteer or NGO. Volunteers can request to join NGOs, and NGOs can approve memberships to build their teams.

2

Report Issues

Volunteers submit sanitation complaints with detailed descriptions, locations, and photos. NGOs receive these reports for review.

3

Assign Tasks

NGOs review complaints, accept those they can handle, and assign specific tasks to their volunteer members.

4

Complete & Verify

Volunteers complete assigned tasks, upload proof of completion, and NGOs verify the work to mark it as complete.
